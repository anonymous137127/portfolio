PT

Hi, I'm Prajwal T N — a BCA student at Sree Siddaganga College of Arts, Science and Commerce, Tumakuru, wrapping up my final year with a focus on software development and cybersecurity.

Most of what I know, I learned by building things that didn't work the first time. That's shown up in projects like FinPulse, a blockchain-secured financial forecasting platform, and an AI cryptocurrency prediction system that combines machine learning with blockchain concepts for data integrity. Both forced me to get comfortable with the unglamorous parts of full-stack work — API design, database schemas, and making a prediction pipeline actually behave in production-like conditions.

Alongside development, I did a short cybersecurity internship at TechnoHacks, where I spent my time scanning networks with Nmap, reading packet captures in Wireshark, and writing up what I found. It changed how I think about the applications I build — less "does this feature work" and more "what happens if someone pokes at it wrong."

Right now I'm looking for an entry-level Software Developer or Full-Stack Developer role where I can keep building things that need to actually hold up — not just demo well.
